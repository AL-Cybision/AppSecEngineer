# Security Policy

Do not open public issues for suspected vulnerabilities. Report them privately to the repository
owner with the affected version, prerequisites, reproduction steps, impact, and supporting
evidence. Do not include live credentials or personal data.

The owner acknowledges critical reports within one business day, validates scope and severity,
assigns remediation ownership, and tracks the issue under the SLAs in `docs/ssdlc.md`. A fix is not
complete until a regression test exists and release evidence is captured. Good-faith testing is
limited to local or explicitly authorized lab environments.
